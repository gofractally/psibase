HOW IT WORKS

Bitcoin, with a clear trail.

  1. Sign in from the account menu. Your signing keys stay in the native wallet.
  2. Deposit Bitcoin. Send BTC to your registered address. Wait for the required confirmations, then submit the transaction ID if a relayer has not credited it.
  3. Use or withdraw. Open your token wallet, or request a Bitcoin withdrawal. Inspect the explorer to see exactly what a payment is waiting for.
